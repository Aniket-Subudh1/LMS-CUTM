# 08 · UI system

Server-first, minimal JavaScript, mobile-first, readable on a slow connection. Students use phones and lab machines; faculty use laptops; admins use desktops.

## 1. Principles

The UI is built on **shadcn/ui** (Radix primitives styled with Tailwind v4), vendored into `components/ui/` at a pinned version. The bar is a professional, consistent product a first-time user understands without training.

1. **Server-first.** Pages are Server Components; interactivity lives in `components/ui/` primitives and the few app client components in §5. A page that only shows data ships no extra JavaScript of its own.
2. **One way to do each thing:** large record lists → `DataTable`; navigation and small lists (fewer than a page of items) → icon card grids; create/edit forms → `FormDialog` (an `ActionForm` + `Field` with shadcn inputs inside a shadcn `Dialog`); filters → `FilterForm`; destructive or one-click actions → `ConfirmSubmit` (shadcn `AlertDialog`); navigation inside the course workspace and Reports → `TabNav`; feedback → the dialog's success state, or an inline `Alert` in the form for errors (no toasts); dashboards → `Card` grids with shadcn charts (M06+).
3. **Not used:** toasts, carousels, infinite scroll, create/edit pages (`/new`, `/[id]/edit`) except the large editors named in 04 §1. Drag-and-drop only to reorder, through `SortableList` (04 §1, M03b). `Sheet` is used only by the mobile sidebar; `AlertDialog` for confirmations and for the message of a row-menu action that failed (`RowMenu`); `Dialog` only through `FormDialog`.
4. **Every page has four states designed:** loading (`loading.tsx` with `Skeleton`s shaped like the page), empty (shadcn `Empty` with a sentence saying what to do next), error (`error.tsx`), not found (`not-found.tsx`).
5. **Icons support words.** Each page has its own generated mark (`PageIcon`, `public/icons/`, the same blue-and-gold clay as the sign-in panel). It is the page's identity: the header, the sidebar item (including Structure's Departments, Programmes and Terms), a card or count that names that page, a section that is a part of the product (outcomes, syllabus, books, batches, attendance), a lesson's type, and the course workspace header and tabs. Phosphor (duotone; regular on a solid fill) stays for controls: a button, a row action, and a status action such as disable or sign out. On a solid fill the duotone layer (`path[opacity]`, a 20% copy of the icon) would be white and read as a smudge, so it is hidden there: every button, whatever its variant (in `button.tsx`; on a white button the layer reads as a grey smudge) and a hovered icon chip that holds a Phosphor icon (`surface-interactive`). A chip that holds a generated mark does not fill: the picture stays on the light chip, and the card's ring tint is the hover. Icon-only buttons are allowed only with an `aria-label` or `sr-only` text (the sidebar trigger, row action menus, collapsed-sidebar items with tooltips).
6. **No double submits, no silent waits.** Every submit button disables and shows a spinner while its action runs, and the form's fields are disabled with it. A confirm dialog stays open with a spinner until its action settles. A sidebar or card link shows a spinner while its navigation is pending (`LinkSpinner`); a list's search box shows one in place of its magnifier while the list loads, and a toolbar without a search box shows one beside its filters (`FilterForm`).
7. **Cards are flat; they do not get shadows or one-sided borders.** A card is `rounded-2xl bg-card ring-1 ring-border/80` on the grey page (the reference designs of 13 Oct 2026). Boxes inside a card (CO cards, facts strips, list items, table rows) are the same white with the ring, so they read as outlined. A clickable card tints its ring with the primary colour and fills its icon chip on hover. Buttons, inputs and selects carry no shadow, and nothing uses violet. No `border-l`/`border-t` accent stripes or inset side shadows anywhere, including the active sidebar item. Dialogs keep `shadow-elevated` to stand off the overlay.
8. **Explain as you go.** Every card has a title and a one-line description of what it is for; every form field has a label and, where a rule applies, a hint; every error says what to do next.

## 3. Navigation

The `(app)` layout follows the reference dashboard Aniket supplied on 13 Oct 2026: a white sidebar and a white sticky top bar around a grey content area whose top-left corner is rounded. ## 6. Page patterns

**List page** — `page.tsx` validates `searchParams` (Zod), calls one DAL list function, renders `PageHeader` (icon, title, description; the "New …" `FormDialog` triggers as `actions`), and either `DataTable` (large lists) or a card grid (small lists such as departments, terms, schemes, the admin landing, my courses). Since 17 Oct 2026 the large admin lists (catalogue, programmes, offerings, audit log, users) pass four counts from their DAL as `stats`, and their `FilterForm` toolbar (a search box and filter pills) and a `bare` `DataTable` as the header's children; My courses puts its toolbar in the header and its card grid below. Every list shows ten rows a page (06 §1).

**Detail page** — validates `params`, calls DAL `get…` (which `notFound()`s on no access), renders sections as `surface` cards with an icon chip, a title and a one-line description. Each section shows its data read-only; its "Edit …" button opens a `FormDialog`. Destructive or one-click actions are `ConfirmSubmit` buttons.

**Create / edit** — a `FormDialog` on the list or detail page. Fields for an edit are pre-filled from the row or record already on the page. On success the action calls `refresh()` (the page behind updates while the success state shows); a create that leads somewhere returns `{ next }` and the dialog navigates there after closing.

**Workspace** — `courses/[offeringId]/layout.tsx` renders a header card (icon chip, offering title, status badge, term and faculty) and `TabNav` inside `<Suspense>`; each tab is its own page with its own `loading.tsx` when it has heavy data.

**Home** — the role-aware dashboard, laid out after the reference dashboard of 13 Oct 2026. “Hello, <first name>”, then two columns from 1280 px (one below). The left column holds the hero, a primary-gradient card featuring the viewer's newest offering (title, term, faculty, status) with a white “Open course” button and a solid primary button for the next step: New offering for admins (it opens that dialog through `?new=`), the offering's Students for faculty and HoDs, All my courses for students, Find a user for support, who has no offerings. For admins it also holds the latest audit entries, each with a coloured entity tile. The right column holds stat tiles from `getHomeSummary()` that link to their list (admin: users and how many are yet to sign in, active offerings, courses, programmes; faculty and HoD: offerings and active enrolments; student: courses); for admins, the audit entries of the last seven IST days as server-rendered bars (seven bars need no Recharts; today's bar is striped and labelled) beside the three people with the most entries that week; and the viewer's next three offerings as `OfferingCard`s. Only data that exists is shown, with no placeholder panels; each milestone adds its own section (M02: “Publish your course plan” for faculty with active offerings that have none, and “Continue learning” for students, both above the tiles; a Notifications tile with the unread count for everyone; today's live classes M03 — live ones first with Join, then the times of the rest of today's, shown only when there are any; an admin sees the ten classes running now in any course, to join one (M03b) — M04: “Due soon” for students (open assessments of their courses closing within a week that they have not handed in, at most five, A-105) and “To grade” for faculty (published assessments of their classes with hand-ins waiting for marks, the five with the most), each only when it has rows; results M08) when it ships. The earlier quick-actions grid, account-security card and role chips are gone: the same links are in the sidebar, the user menu and each list page's own buttons.

**Role-aware page** — one route serves several roles; the DAL returns a discriminated DTO (`{ view: 'student', … } | { view: 'teacher', … }`) and the page renders the matching section. Do not create parallel routes per role.

## 7. Content and formatting

- Sentence case for headings and buttons ("Publish results", not "Publish Results").
- Dates: `12 Oct 2026, 10:30 AM` in IST via `formatDateTime()`; relative times only for "due in 3 h" badges (`formatDue()`); both in `lib/format.ts`.
- Marks: `36 / 50`; percentages with up to 2 decimals and no trailing zeros (`74%`, `66.67%`).
- Empty states say what to do: "No lessons yet. Add the first lesson to Module 1."
- Error messages come from `AppError` messages (06 §1); never show stack traces or vendor text.

## 8. Accessibility and responsiveness

- Every input has a visible `<label>` (via `Field`). Errors are associated text, not colour alone.
- Keyboard: everything reachable and operable; visible focus rings come from the shadcn primitives (`focus-visible:ring`); Radix menus and dialogs handle focus and `Esc`.
- Contrast: WCAG 2.1 AA for text and badges.
- `aria-live="polite"` for the attempt timer's minute updates and autosave status; `role="alert"` for form errors.
- Layout works from 360 px wide (no horizontal page scroll — e2e asserts it). Tables scroll horizontally inside their container on small screens; the attempt runner is single-column on phones.
- Low bandwidth (NFR-07): read pages are server-rendered HTML; uploads show progress and can be retried; videos stream from object storage/CDN with `preload="metadata"`.
